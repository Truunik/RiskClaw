RiskClaw decides — in real time and verifiably — how a Uniswap v4-style pool should behave under risk: ALLOW, PENALTY_FEE, or BLOCK.

An off-chain agent swarm reasons. A TEE attests. The hook enforces. Nothing the AI says reaches the swap path until it survives deterministic guardrails and is committed onchain alongside fetchable proof roots.

The shape of the proof

explanationRoot + computeProofRoot + metricsRoot + promptHash + modelHash + provider + responseIdHash + verifiedAt — all stored in the registry's PolicyProof struct.

Click 0G Compute to see the TEE-verified inference call. Click RiskHook to see how the policy lands at swap time.
